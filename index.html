01 / ABOUT

Research grounded in systems thinking.

I investigate how intelligent orchestration can help distributed edge systems respond to changing workloads, resource constraints, and network conditions.

My research combines reinforcement learning, federated learning, graph neural networks, privacy-aware optimization, and trace-driven simulation. I aim to evaluate not only performance gains, but also their trade-offs in latency, energy use, communication overhead, reliability, and scalability.

FEDSEM GNN / EDGE ORCHESTRATION

FEATURED PROJECT

FedSemGNN

A simulation-only research framework for semantic-aware federated reinforcement learning in 6G edge orchestration. The public repository is the starting point for code, reproducibility notes, and future releases.

  • Semantic context and graph-based topology representation
  • Hierarchical reinforcement learning for orchestration
  • Evaluation focused on performance and communication trade-offs

Code, trained models, derived inputs, and experimental results for graph-neural thermal prediction and cooling allocation in a simulated server cluster. The release includes reproducibility materials and both PyTorch and NumPy/SciPy implementations.
